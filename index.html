Ask a Question

PTFE (Polytetrafluoroethylene), commonly known as Teflon, is a synthetic fluoropolymer known for exceptional chemical resistance, heat resistance up to 260 °C and a very low coefficient of friction. That combination makes it the go-to material for gaskets, seals, insulation and anti-stick applications across virtually every industry.

Yes. We provide precision custom cutting and CNC machining to your exact specifications. Share drawings, dimensions or a sample and our team will produce components to tight tolerances.

Standard stock orders are dispatched within 2–3 business days. Custom machined orders take 5–10 business days depending on complexity and volume. We work with trusted logistics partners for safe, timely delivery across all Indian states.

Yes. Our products meet international standards including ISO 9001:2015, FDA compliance for food and pharmaceutical applications, RoHS compliance and REACH certification. Full documentation and test certificates are provided with every order.

We stock virgin PTFE (highest purity) plus glass-filled, carbon-filled, bronze-filled and graphite-filled grades. Each offers specific advantages in wear resistance, thermal conductivity or mechanical strength — our team will recommend the right grade for your application.
