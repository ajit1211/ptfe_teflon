Common Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED

PTFE (Polytetrafluoroethylene), commonly known as Teflon, is a synthetic fluoropolymer renowned for its exceptional resistance to chemicals, heat (up to 260°C), and low friction coefficient. It's the go-to material for gaskets, seals, insulation, and anti-stick applications across virtually every industry.
Absolutely. We provide precision custom cutting and CNC machining services to meet your exact specifications. Simply share your drawings, dimensions, or samples and our engineering team will produce components to tight tolerances.
Standard stock orders are dispatched within 2–3 business days. Custom machined orders may take 5–10 business days depending on complexity and volume. We work with trusted logistics partners to ensure safe, timely delivery across all Indian states.
Yes. Our products meet international standards including ISO 9001:2015, FDA compliance for food and pharmaceutical applications, RoHS compliance, and REACH certification. We provide full documentation and test certificates with every order.
We stock virgin PTFE (highest purity), glass-filled, carbon-filled, bronze-filled, and graphite-filled PTFE grades. Each grade offers specific properties for wear resistance, thermal conductivity, or mechanical strength. Our team will recommend the right grade for your application.
